Principles

How we think.

We don't publish a playbook. But we hold a small number of beliefs with total conviction — and they govern every decision we make, every venture we build, every partner we choose.

  • 01 Output is noise. Outcomes are everything. We don't celebrate shipping. We celebrate what changed in the world as a result.
  • 02 The hardest unknown first. Always. Every initiative has one assumption whose failure makes everything else irrelevant. We find it and test it before anything else.
  • 03 10x ambition or don't start. Incremental improvements fight incumbents on their home ground. Radical solutions create entirely new terrain.
  • 04 Learning velocity is our primary currency. The speed at which we generate validated knowledge matters more than the speed at which we ship. Fast learning compounds.
  • 05 Kill efficiently. Kill without shame. Terminating a bad idea fast is a professional achievement. We build kill criteria before we build anything else.
  • 06 Empowerment bounded by context. We set the outcome. Our people and partners own the path. Autonomy without strategic context is chaos.
