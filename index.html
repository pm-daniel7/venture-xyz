Principles

How we think
about the work.

We don't publish a methodology. But we hold a small number of beliefs with complete conviction — and they govern every decision we make inside a team.

  • 01 Output is noise. Outcomes are the only measure. We don't celebrate shipping. We celebrate what changed in the world as a result of shipping. The distance between the two is where most product work gets lost.
  • 02 Test the hardest assumption first. Every initiative has one assumption whose failure makes everything else irrelevant. We find it before we build anything else — not because it's comfortable, but because it's the only way to work efficiently.
  • 03 Radical thinking, not incremental tweaking. We ask whether there's a fundamentally better approach before we optimise the current one. Small improvements keep you competitive. Radical ones make the competition irrelevant.
  • 04 Learning fast is the real competitive advantage. The team that validates ideas cheapest and fastest wins — not the team that ships most. We build the learning habit into the cadence of every engagement.
  • 05 Kill bad ideas quickly, without ceremony. Stopping work on a failing idea is not failure — it's the right decision made at the right time. We establish exit criteria before we start, so the decision is never emotional.
  • 06 Accountability over authority. We don't come in to tell your team what to do. We come in to own outcomes alongside them. The work is shared. The responsibility is ours.
